# Brag Plan: Dev Fashion

## What is this app?
Dev Fashion is a Coimbatore bridal-couture atelier offering South Indian bridal blouse design, custom tailoring, and hand-embroidered made-to-measure looks.

## The angle
A bridal editorial film that treats Dev Fashion's real portfolio like an heirloom collection: warm ivory, ink-dark frames, antique gold, and close, deliberate reveals. The site is a couture atelier rather than a photography booking service, so the film celebrates its bridal designs and the gallery experience without claiming photography services.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Open on a real bridal-look detail, revealed from near-black, with the site's signature line: “Crafting Elegance, Designed for You.”

## Key moments (the middle)
- Reveal the real South Indian bridal blouse designer identity and Coimbatore studio location.
- Recreate the site's portfolio carousel as an editorial lookbook, then open a selected “Bridal Couture” look.
- Hold on the site's made-to-measure promise over a real portfolio photograph.

## Outro / punchline
Dev Fashion's original wordmark resolves in a final gold-on-ivory signature card, with “Crafting Elegance, Designed for You” and its official website.

## User flow worth showing
Landing-page hero → browse the 20-look portfolio carousel → open a bridal look and see its made-to-measure details.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: a quiet, luxurious South Indian bridal editorial; heritage craft photographed like a modern fashion-house campaign
- Interpretation: dramatic but not loud; five image-led scenes, measured camera motion, high-contrast serif titles, generous holds, and no voiceover.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920 (9:16, Instagram Reel)
## Duration: 21 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #FAF8F5, with #0C0C0B for selected dark frames
- Accent: #B38E46 and #C5A059
- Text: #141312
- Display font: Cormorant Garamond
- Body font: Manrope
- Strongest visual element: the live site's 3D bridal-look carousel and look-detail viewer, grounded in real portfolio photographs and the original wordmark.

## Share copy (draft)
Dev Fashion. Crafting Elegance, Designed for You.

## Audio direction
- Role: restrained cinematic support beneath the real bridal imagery
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, warm and steady at a low level
- Music treatment: begin with a gentle fade-in, keep the bed around 0.30, and fade out beneath the final brand card; prioritize the image and legibility over beat-matching.
- Music cue guidance: bundled vol-12 cue preset at about 110 BPM; strong cues at 8.74s, 13.11s, and 17.47s may support the portfolio, detail, and brand reveals. The grid is about 0.55s; use cues as optional guidance, not a reason to rush readable text.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none unless the local HyperFrames audio-data workflow is available; no visualizer graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse and soft; a restrained reveal accent and a delicate final logo accent only if they fit the motion.
- Audio-coupled moments: the portfolio selection and final brand reveal may land near the strong cues.
- Restraint rule: no dramatic impacts, bright repeated clicks, voiceover, or audio that competes with the bridal photographs.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The detail — 4.20s
Begin in near-black, then reveal a real close bridal-detail photograph. The exact site line “Crafting Elegance, Designed for You” appears with a soft, editorial title reveal and stays settled long enough to read.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: an intimate music fade-in; no sharp hit.
Audio-coupled idea: let the first image emerge with the music rather than a beat-locked title.
Music: low, warm bed begins at the opening.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — The atelier — 4.54s
Reveal a real bridal look from the site's portfolio, framed like a fashion-house campaign. Add the source wording “South Indian Bridal Blouse Designer” and “Coimbatore”; do not invent credentials or service claims.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: widen the score as the full look arrives.
Audio-coupled idea: a quiet, low-risk reveal accent may support the photograph's arrival.
Music: continue under the image.
Transition mood: clean editorial match cut → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — The lookbook — 4.37s
Show an editorial recreation of the site's actual 3D portfolio carousel with three real photographs. Select one card and reveal the site's “Look 01 / 20” and “Bridal Couture” detail labels.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three portfolio cards enter in order, then the center “Bridal Couture” look opens; settle the full label set before the cut.
Audio intent: one restrained accent for the selection, not a sound on every card.
Audio-coupled idea: align the selected-look reveal near the 8.74s strong cue; keep text holds readable.
Music: continue, with the reveal allowed to land near a strong cue.
Transition mood: selected-look match cut → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Made to measure — 4.36s
Move into a second real portfolio image with visible embroidery and fabric detail. Hold the site's exact look-viewer copy: “Hand-embroidered and made to measure in our Coimbatore atelier.”
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: keep the music spacious; let the image carry the moment.
Audio-coupled idea: let the copy settle near the 13.11s cue without moving once readable.
Music: continue under the text and image.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — The signature — 3.53s
Resolve to a clean ivory brand card with the original Dev Fashion wordmark, the exact signature “Crafting Elegance, Designed for You,” and the official website “www.devfashion.in.” Keep the tagline settled for at least two seconds.
Sequential/interaction: the original wordmark lands first; the signature and official website follow, then hold.
Audio intent: restrained final accent, then let the music fade with the card.
Audio-coupled idea: aim the brand-name reveal near 17.47s; preserve the final reading hold.
Music: fade out beneath the end card.
Transition mood: quiet fade to ivory.

**Music mood for this video:** warm, restrained, cinematic.
**Audio summary:** A soft, steady bed opens with the bridal detail, broadens for the lookbook, and fades under the final signature; only a couple of quiet, motion-matched accents are considered.
